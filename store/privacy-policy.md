# Privacy Policy — Bubble Pop!

**Last updated:** October 7, 2026

Bubble Pop! ("the app") is developed by Muhammad Uzair.

## Data collection
The app does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It works
fully offline and contains no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking
tools, or user accounts.

## Permissions
The app requests no sensitive device permissions.

## Children's privacy
Because no data is collected from anyone, the app is safe for all ages,
including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes (for example, if online features are added in a
future version), the updated policy will be published at the store listing
URL before the update rolls out.
